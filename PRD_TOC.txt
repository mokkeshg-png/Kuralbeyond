27.	Speaking	Practice
1.	
Model	audio	plays.
2.	
Learner	taps	“Record.”
3.	
Microphone	permission	requested	(with	plain-language	explanation).
4.	
Recording	captured	(max	10	seconds).
5.	
Playback:	learner’s	voice	→	model	audio.
6.	
Self-assessment	prompt:	“How	close	was	your	pronunciation?”
7.	
Recording	deleted	from	server	after	24	hours	(or	immediately	if	not	saved).

92.	Assumptions
1.	
The	hackathon	team	has	4	members	with	basic	web	development	skills.
2.	
Supabase	free	tier	is	sufficient	for	MVP.
3.	
Prerecorded	audio	can	be	sourced	from	team	members	or	free	libraries.
4.	
Tamil	content	can	be	reviewed	by	a	native	speaker	on	the	team	or	a	volunteer.
5.	
The	hackathon	provides	internet	access	and	deployment	platform	access.
6.	
Judges	evaluate	based	on	working	demo,	not	production	readiness.
7.	
English-speaking	children	abroad	are	the	primary	audience.
8.	
Tanglish	is	a	familiar	concept	to	the	target	audience’s	families.
9.	
Progressive	Tanglish	reduction	is	pedagogically	sound	(requiring	validation).
10.	
AI	features	are	optional	enhancements,	not	core	requirements.
