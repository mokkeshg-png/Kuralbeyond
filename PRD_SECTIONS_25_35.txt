27.	Speaking	Practice
Scope	Limitation

=== PAGE 11 ===
Important:
	Phoneme-level	pronunciation	assessment	for	Tamil	is	not	commercially	reliable	at	MVP	scale.	Tamil	speech	recognition	models
achieve	WER	of	0.55–0.71	on	dialectal	speech,	and	children’s	speech	performs	worse.	Therefore:
P0:
	Speaking	practice	uses	
record-and-compare
	(learner	records,	hears	their	voice	alongside	model	audio)	without	AI	scoring.
P1:
	Optional	speech-to-text	transcription	via	Web	Speech	API	or	third-party	service,	presented	as	“speech	recognition”	not	“pronunciation
assessment.”
P2:
	Phoneme-level	assessment	if	a	validated	Tamil	assessment	API	becomes	available.
Recording	Flow
1.	
Model	audio	plays.
2.	
Learner	taps	“Record.”
3.	
Microphone	permission	requested	(with	plain-language	explanation).
4.	
Recording	captured	(max	10	seconds).
5.	
Playback:	learner’s	voice	→	model	audio.
6.	
Self-assessment	prompt:	“How	close	was	your	pronunciation?”
7.	
Recording	deleted	from	server	after	24	hours	(or	immediately	if	not	saved).
Consent	and	Privacy
Microphone	access	requires	explicit	consent.
For	users	under	13,	parental	consent	required	before	any	recording.
Recordings	are	never	shared,	used	for	training,	or	retained	beyond	24	hours.
Recording	feature	can	be	disabled	by	parent/guardian	in	settings.
